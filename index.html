Reading Passage 1

Is your child at school today?

Practice passage · General Training Reading

Forcing a child to go to school has always been a controversial issue. In the past, many children were needed at home to help with farm work or to earn money for their families. Nowadays, however, almost every country in the world has laws requiring children to go to school, at least until a certain age.

There are several reasons for compulsory education. Firstly, it helps to ensure that all children receive a basic level of education, which is considered important for their future lives. Secondly, schools provide a safe and structured environment, where children can develop social skills and learn to live and work with others. Finally, education is seen as essential for the economic and social development of a country.

However, some people still believe that parents should have the right to decide whether their child goes to school. They argue that it is a family's responsibility, and that compulsory education interferes with personal freedom. Others suggest that the school system is not suitable for all children and that some may learn better at home or through alternative forms of education.

Despite these debates, most governments continue to support compulsory education. They believe that the benefits for individuals and society as a whole are greater than any disadvantages.

HOLIDAY APARTMENTS TO LET

From a travel magazine article

More and more people are choosing to stay in holiday apartments when they travel. These apartments are often cheaper than hotels and give you more space and freedom. You can cook your own meals, which can save money, and you can live like a local, rather than feeling like a tourist.

Holiday apartments are available in many different places, from busy cities to quiet countryside villages. Some are in modern buildings with facilities such as swimming pools and gyms, while others are in traditional houses full of character.

Before booking an apartment, consider its location, the facilities available and the terms of the rental agreement. Read recent reviews and check what is included in the price. Cleaning fees, deposits and cancellation conditions can vary, so it is worth checking these details in advance.

Recruitment Information

When applying for a job, candidates should read the job description carefully and make sure their experience matches the role. A clear application should explain relevant skills and give examples of previous achievements.

Employers may invite shortlisted candidates to an interview. Applicants should arrive on time, prepare questions about the position and bring any requested documents. After the interview, a brief thank-you message can be a professional way to express continued interest.

Workplace training helps employees understand policies, develop practical skills and work safely. New employees should ask for clarification whenever instructions are unclear.

Reading Passage 3

The changing world of work

Technology has changed the way many people work. Digital tools make it possible for teams to collaborate across different locations, while flexible schedules can help some employees balance work and family responsibilities.

However, remote work also presents challenges. Workers may feel isolated, and communication can be more difficult when colleagues rarely meet in person. Organisations can address these issues by setting clear expectations, scheduling regular check-ins and supporting staff wellbeing.

The most effective working arrangements depend on the type of job, the needs of employees and the goals of the organisation. There is no single approach that works for everyone.
